Prey animals

White-tailed deer

Prey · ~70 kg · skittish, flees at ~40 m, bolts in zig-zag. Yields venison, hide, bone, sinew.

CC BY 4.0 · Alex AbairPublic domain · NPS PhotoPublic domain · Shenandoah National Park from Virginia

Elk / Wapiti

Big prey · ~300 kg · herds, bulls charge when cornered in rut (autumn).

CC BY-SA 4.0 · Frank SchulenburgCC BY 2.0 · James St. JohnCC BY 2.0 · James St. John

Wild boar

Prey that fights back · ~90 kg · charges if hurt, tusks deal heavy damage.

CC BY-SA 4.0 · Dominicus Johannes BergsmaCC BY-SA 4.0 · Dominicus Johannes BergsmaCC BY-SA 2.0 · Evelyn Simak

Snowshoe hare

Small prey · coat turns white in winter. Quick meat, small fur pelt.

CC BY-SA 3.0 · Lepus_americanus_5459.JPG: Walter Siegmund (talk) derivativePublic domain · Colin Canterbury/USFWSPublic domain · The original uploader was Conti at English Wikipedia.

Wild turkey

Small prey · flocks, flushes when approached. Feathers for arrow fletching.
